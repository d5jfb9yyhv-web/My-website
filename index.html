About the studio

Design that makes people stop,
look and trust your brand.

Novadee Studios is an independent design studio based in Delhi NCR, founded by Abhishek Sinha. We create clean, modern visuals for creators, startups and small businesses — work that stands out and is delivered on time.

Every project starts with your goal, not a template. Whether you’re a YouTuber growing a channel, a startup preparing a pitch or a local brand launching a new menu, the aim is the same: a design your audience notices in the first second and remembers after.

  • Brand identity

    Logos and visual identities that feel premium at every size.

  • YouTube

    Thumbnails, banners and channel art built to earn the click.

  • Social media

    Instagram posts and campaigns with a consistent, polished look.

  • Ads & presentations

    Typographic ads, posters and decks that sell the idea.
